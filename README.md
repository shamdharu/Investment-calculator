---

## About the Project

The Investment Calculator helps you estimate how your investments could grow over time. It allows you to explore how monthly contributions, investment returns, time, and inflation can affect your future wealth.

Use it to understand the power of consistency, visualize your financial goals, and plan for the future.

## Features

- Calculate the estimated future value of regular investments.
- Explore different monthly investment amounts and time periods.
- Adjust the expected annual investment return.
- Account for inflation to estimate future purchasing power.
- Compare your projected investment value with its value in today's money.

## Remember

These calculations are estimates, not guarantees. Actual investment returns and inflation can change over time.

You don't have to become wealthy overnight. Start by understanding yourself
